03 / Conduct

The Line We Hold

Every engagement is premised on one position: no trafficking, no coercion, no debt servitude. A business built on coerced labour is not a client - it is a crime scene, and we point it at the authorities instead of advising it. This is not a disclaimer. It is the reason the rest of the practice is worth running.

Sex work is decriminalised in NSW. The work itself is lawful; the abuse around it is what the law prosecutes. Our advice exists to keep an operation on the lawful side of that line - protected workers, a compliant premises, and nothing hidden.
