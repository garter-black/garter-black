02 · The Wiring

Built for uplink.

Most rooms in this city are sold for sleeping. Ours are wired for work.

iDual Fibre

2.2 Gbps x 2. Two independent upstreams into the core. If one carrier blinks, the building does not.

iiThe Hardline

Cat6a, Cat7 and Cat8 ethernet, extendable to every streaming room. A wall port, not a password.

iiiWireless

WiFi 7 across the 6 GHz band on every floor. No dead zones. Anywhere in the building.

ivThe Wall

Wall-scale 4K displays as standard fit-out. A room that reads like a set, because for most of the people in it, it is one.
